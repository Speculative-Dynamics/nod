Questions.

Good to know.

Is Nod a therapist?

No. Nod is a good listener, not a therapist, doctor, or emergency service. If you're struggling, please talk to someone you trust. If something you write sounds like you might be in danger, Nod shows a helpline you can call.

Does it need the internet?

No. Once Nod is set up, it works fully offline. On some iPhones there's a one-time download first (see below).

Which iPhones does it work on?

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Nod works straight away. On other iPhones with at least 6 GB of memory, such as iPhone 13 Pro, iPhone 14 and newer, Nod downloads its own AI once (about 2–3 GB over Wi-Fi). It needs iOS 26 or later.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no account, and nothing to buy inside the app.

Can anyone else read what I write?

Not through Nod. Your conversation is saved only on your iPhone, and we never receive it. Turn on Face ID in Nod's settings to keep it locked. iCloud backup of your conversation is off unless you turn it on.

How do I delete what I've told it?

Open What Nod remembers to forget any note, or tap Start fresh in settings to delete the whole conversation. Deleting the app removes everything too.
