Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Effective immediately.

The short version

Nod runs entirely on your iPhone. We don't have a server that sees what you write. We don't have accounts. We don't track you, profile you, or analyse you. Your conversation stays local unless you explicitly turn on iCloud backup for your own account, which is off by default.

This isn't a promise we ask you to trust — it's an architecture choice you can verify. The app's source is public at github.com/Speculative-Dynamics/nod.

Who we are

Nod (listed on the App Store as Just Nod) is built and published by Speculative Dynamics Private Limited, a company incorporated in India ("Speculative Dynamics", "we", "us", or "our"). This policy applies to the Nod iOS app and the marketing website at usenod.app.

What we process, and where

The Nod iOS app

Everything you do in Nod — what you type or say, Nod's replies, its memory notes, what you tell it about yourself, and your settings — is processed and stored on your iPhone. Specifically:

  • Replies. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Nod uses Apple's on-device model (FoundationModels). On other iPhones, or if you pick one in advanced settings, it uses an open model that runs on the phone instead: Qwen 3.5 2B, or Qwen 3.5 4B on iPhones with 8 GB of memory. Your messages are never sent to a server to generate a reply.
  • Voice input. When you tap the microphone, Apple's on-device speech recognition turns your voice into text on your iPhone. Nod asks for microphone and speech recognition permission the first time. Audio is not stored or sent to us.
  • Memory. Nod writes short notes about earlier parts of your conversation and looks back through your own earlier messages on the device to recall details. Notes are shown in What Nod remembers, where you can forget any of them.
  • What you tell Nod about yourself (the Anything else note) and your reply preferences are stored on your iPhone and read by the on-device model with each message.
  • Your conversation is stored in a database on your iPhone, protected by iOS data encryption. If you turn on Require Face ID, Nod asks for Face ID before showing it, and hides it in the App Switcher.
  • iCloud backup is off by default. If you turn on Back up conversation and memory, your messages, memory notes and what you've told Nod about yourself are included in your own iCloud backup, handled by Apple under your Apple Account settings. We can't access it.
  • Start fresh permanently deletes your conversation and memory notes on that device. This can't be undone. Your Anything else note stays until you clear it. Deleting the app removes everything.
  • Helplines. If something you write suggests you might be in danger, Nod shows a helpline for your region. This check happens on your iPhone; nothing is reported to anyone.

When the app uses the network

Nod never sends your conversation anywhere. It only uses the network in these cases:

  • Downloading an AI model. If Nod uses a Qwen model, it downloads the model files (about 1.7–3 GB) once, from our model storage on Cloudflare R2. Like any download, this shares your IP address and basic request details with Cloudflare. It contains nothing about you or your conversation. Downloads wait for Wi-Fi unless you turn on Download over cellular. After that, Nod works fully offline. You can delete a downloaded model in advanced settings.
  • Apple's own downloads. iOS may download Apple Intelligence or speech recognition files the first time they're needed. Apple handles these under its own privacy policy.
  • Things you choose to open. Send feedback opens your Mail app with a draft to us that includes the Nod version, iOS version and iPhone model; nothing is sent unless you send it. Helpline Call and Text buttons open your Phone or Messages app, and Find a helpline opens findahelpline.com in your browser.

The website (usenod.app)

This website is a set of static pages with no forms, no sign-in, no cookies and no analytics. Its fonts and scripts are served from usenod.app itself, so no other service is contacted while you browse it. Like any website, our static hosting provider, which serves the pages, sees standard request details (your IP address, browser, the page requested and the time).

We use none of this to identify, profile or track you, and we don't share or sell it. The hosting provider handles these details under its own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't create user accounts.
  • We don't collect or process your message content.
  • We don't use analytics, telemetry, crash reporting services or tracking pixels.
  • We don't embed third-party SDKs in the app for ads, attribution, or A/B testing.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share any personal data — because we don't have any to share.
  • We don't train models on your data.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) — and analogous laws including the EU GDPR and the California CCPA — you have rights of access, correction, erasure, and portability over your personal data. Because we don't collect personal data, most of these rights are moot: there is nothing on our systems to access, correct, export, or delete.

For the data on your own device, you exercise these rights yourself by using the app's "Start fresh" button (to erase) or by deleting the app (to remove everything).

If you believe we hold personal data about you — or you have a question about this policy — you can reach us at hello@usenod.app and we'll respond within a reasonable period (typically under 30 days).

Children

Nod is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Because the app operates entirely on-device and does not require an account, age is not verified at the app level.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, note it in the app's release notes and on the website homepage.
