Support

Most questions answer themselves once you remember Nod runs entirely on your phone.

Get in touch

The fastest path is email: hello@usenod.app. In the app, Send feedback in settings opens Mail with a draft to us that already includes your Nod version, iOS version and iPhone model. We read every message and reply within a few business days. For bug reports with logs or screenshots, the GitHub issue tracker is also open.

Because Nod has no servers, we genuinely cannot see your conversations or device state. To help us reproduce something, please describe what happened in your own words (and, if you're not using Send feedback, include your iPhone model and iOS version). Don't paste conversation content unless it's directly relevant — we'd rather not have it.

Common questions

Which iPhones does Nod work on?

Nod needs iOS 26 or later. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro and newer), it works straight away. Other iPhones with at least 6 GB of memory, such as iPhone 13 Pro and iPhone 14, download Nod's own AI once. iPhones with less memory can't run Nod, and the app will say so.

Nod says it's downloading. What's happening?

On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, Nod downloads its own AI the first time you open it: about 1.7 GB, or 3 GB if you choose the larger model in advanced settings. It waits for Wi-Fi unless you allow cellular (Settings → Show advanced settings → Download over cellular). Progress shows above the message box. Once it's done, it never downloads again.

Why does the download seem stuck?

Usually it's waiting for Wi-Fi, the network is blocking large downloads, or iOS is holding downloads back in Low Power Mode. Plug your iPhone in and connect to Wi-Fi; the download picks up where it left off.

Where are my conversations stored?

Only on your iPhone, encrypted by iOS. Nothing is uploaded to us. If you turn on Back up conversation and memory in Nod's settings, it's included in your own iCloud backup under your Apple account. It's off unless you turn it on.

How do I delete everything?

Inside the app, tap Start fresh in settings. That permanently deletes your conversation and everything Nod remembers on this device. Your Anything else note stays, so clear that box too if you want it gone. To remove just one memory, open What Nod remembers and forget that note. Deleting the app removes everything.

Can I change how Nod replies?

Yes. Under How Nod talks in settings, choose a reply length (Brief, Conversational or Deeper) and an approach (Listen, Reflect back or Offer perspective). In Anything else, add a few sentences about yourself, like "I work night shifts". Nod reads these with every message.

How do I free up space?

If Nod downloaded its own AI, you can delete models you're not using in settings → Show advanced settings. Deleting one means downloading it again if you switch back to it.

Does Nod work offline?

Yes. Once Nod is set up (and, on some iPhones, its AI has downloaded once), it works fully offline. Try it in Airplane Mode.

Can I use a different language?

Nod's AI understands many languages, so you can write in the one you're most comfortable with. The app's buttons and menus are in English for now.

Voice input doesn't seem to work.

Make sure you've granted microphone and speech-recognition permissions when prompted. If you skipped those prompts, re-enable them in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone (and the same path for Speech Recognition). Your voice is turned into text on your iPhone. Voice isn't available in every language yet; you can always type instead.

How do I turn on Face ID?

In settings, toggle Require Face ID. After that, opening the app prompts for Face ID before the conversation appears. Useful when you share your phone or hand it around.

Is Nod a substitute for professional help?

No. Nod is a general-purpose AI chat app — a calm conversation partner, not a counselor, doctor, or therapist. If you're in a crisis or need professional support, please contact local emergency services or a qualified professional in your country.

What happens if I write something worrying?

If something you write suggests you might be in danger, Nod shows a free helpline for your region with buttons to call or text it, plus a link to findahelpline.com for other countries. This check happens on your iPhone. Nobody is alerted and nothing is sent anywhere.

Is Nod really open source?

Yes. The full Swift source — the UI, the model wrappers, the system prompts, the database schema — is on GitHub at github.com/Speculative-Dynamics/nod under the MIT license. The build on the App Store is signed from the maintainer's account but built from the code in the repo.

I have a feature request.

Open an issue on GitHub with the enhancement label, or email hello@usenod.app. We can't promise everything ships, but we read everything.

Reporting a security issue

If you find a security or privacy issue, please email hello@usenod.app with the subject "Security" and a clear description. Please don't open a public GitHub issue for security problems before we've had a chance to look. Our full disclosure policy is in SECURITY.md.
